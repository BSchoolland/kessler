KESSLER

a sword. a handful of planets. gravity does the rest.

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE WASD / left stick — you walk along the planet you're standing on. The sword faces where you move
SWING left click / RT — arc in front of you. Hits knock enemies away. Swing while flying fast for a long dive-slash
DASH space, shift or right click / LT — goes where you're moving and always lifts you off. Invulnerable while dashing
DASH-STRIKE swing right after a dash for 2× damage and a much harder launch
FLY WASD in the air steers you but burns fuel (bar under HP). Fuel refills on the ground. Dashing is free
GUN Q / E / scroll / Y swaps. Auto-aim locks a target, Tab / R / X cycles. Rounds are earned with sword hits

Knockback is the whole game. Launched enemies take impact damage when they hit a planet, bowl into their friends, or fly past the red ring and are lost to the void.

Every kill shatters into debris that keeps orbiting and keeps hitting things — including you. The better you're doing, the messier it gets. Swing at debris to bat it.

Enemies arrive by pod. Orbiters circle a planet and shoot; swing at their shots to send them back.
