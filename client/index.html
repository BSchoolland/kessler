KESSLER

a sword. a handful of planets. gravity does the rest.

HOW TO PLAY

MOVE WASD, arrows or left stick — you walk along the planet you're standing on, whichever way you push, and face the way you move. Standing still you face up
ATTACK space, click or RT. On a planet it's melee: a wide sweep over your head when you're standing still, a wave along the ground when you're moving. Hits knock enemies away
LAUNCH shift, right click or LT — leaves the planet where you're moving. Only from the ground, no cooldown, needs fuel. Invulnerable during the burst
LAUNCH-STRIKE attack right after a launch for 2× damage and a much harder knockback
FLY WASD in space steers you but burns fuel (gauge by the ship). Fuel refills on the ground; an empty tank still gives a short push once a second
GUN In space attack fires the gun, auto-aimed at the nearest enemy. Standing on a planet reloads one round every 2s. Dry gun in space: attack is a pulse that bats shots away

WHO'S WHO

Knockback is the whole game. Launched enemies take impact damage when they hit a planet, bowl into their friends, or fly past the red ring and are lost to the void.

Every kill shatters into debris that keeps orbiting and keeps hitting things — including you. The better you're doing, the messier it gets. Swing at debris to bat it.

Enemies arrive by pod. Orbiters circle a planet and shoot, and every so often hop over to yours. The Flak parks on a planet you're not on and fires rockets straight up that bend toward you. Raiders never land: they patrol the outer ring and fire spreads inward. Lancers telegraph, then charge along the ground. Mines drift in and go off near you; hit one first and it's your bomb. Splitters break into two hoppers. Sweepers land on your planet and run a low laser around it: hop over it, or knock the turret off the ground. The Aegis carries a shield that turns toward you slowly: slugs and the wave break on it, the sweep and its back don't care. Bombers orbit your planet and drop bombs that burst along the ground where they land. Your wave and your sweep both send shots back.

A boss every five waves, four of them in a row: the Hammer, the Warden, the Twins, the Belt. Beat the Belt at wave 20 and the run is won; it keeps going as long as you do.
